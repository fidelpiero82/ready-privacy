# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Ready? Ready! ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## Information We Collect

When using Ready? Ready!, we may collect:

- Email address (for authentication and account management)
- Child profiles you create
- Clothing lists and inventory
- Calendar events and reminders
- Invitations sent to caregivers
- Subscription status (Google Play)

We do not collect sensitive personal information unless you voluntarily provide it.

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

- Provide the services of the app
- Synchronize data across your devices
- Share information with caregivers you invite
- Manage Premium subscriptions
- Improve the app

## Data Storage

Data is securely stored using Supabase cloud services.

## Sharing Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information is only shared with caregivers that you explicitly invite.

## Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play Billing.

We never receive or store your payment information.

## Children's Privacy

Ready? Ready! is intended for parents and caregivers.

The app stores information about children only as entered voluntarily by parents or legal guardians.

## Your Rights

You may update or delete your information at any time.
